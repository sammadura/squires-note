Hi. I'm the new hire.

I start whenever you say hello. Strange for someone you haven't met, so here's the deal.

I'll spend about an hour asking about the business the way you'd tell anyone on their first day. What happens every week. What costs you the most. Where everything lives. What you'd never want me doing on my own.

Then I'll connect to the tools you already use, build my own notes in this folder, and do one real job with you before the hour's up.

That's only day one. Every job after it, I get better at yours, and if you fix me once, it stays fixed. Every go you give me is trust earned. One day you'll hand me the keys. Until then, I ask.

Nothing gets sent, posted, or paid for while we do it. I draft. You say go.
